# The Little Baking Troll Canva Templates

These templates are 1080 x 1350 Instagram portrait designs, ready to upload into Canva.

## Templates

1. `01-recipe-reveal.svg` - New recipe announcement with a large photo placeholder.
2. `02-ingredients-list.svg` - Ingredient list post for recipe previews or grocery-save content.
3. `03-baking-tip.svg` - Educational baking tip post.
4. `04-carousel-cover.svg` - Carousel cover for recipe steps, tips, or mini tutorials.
5. `05-cozy-quote.svg` - Cozy community/quote post for engagement.
6. `skincare-vitamin-c-serum-launch.svg` - Minimal skincare launch post with soft peach, white, bold serif headings, and a product-photo placeholder.
7. `fitness-bootcamp-weekend-flyer.svg` - Vibrant orange and black weekend fitness bootcamp flyer with bold typography and schedule space.

## How To Use In Canva

1. Open Canva.
2. Create an Instagram portrait design, 1080 x 1350 px.
3. Upload one of the SVG files.
4. Place it on the canvas and resize to fill the page.
5. Edit text, colors, and add your recipe photo over the placeholder area.
